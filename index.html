Tools available on the platform

Live and in-progress digital tools supporting each group involved in the project, grouped by who uses them day to day.

Teachers

  • Blended Lesson BuilderLive
    Templates for combining classroom and online instruction, aligned to the RTB curriculum.
  • Digital Pedagogy HubLive
    Self-paced training modules and coaching resources for delivering blended learning.
  • Offline Content SyncWatch
    Downloads lesson packages for use where connectivity is unreliable, syncs when back online.

Students

  • TVET Skills PortalLive
    Student-facing access point for course material, practical assignments and progress tracking.
  • Employability TrackerLive
    Tracks practical, digital and soft-skill milestones tied to labour-market requirements.
  • Peer Practice LabsBuilding
    Guided exercise sets for hands-on digital skills practice between class sessions.

RTB & RTTI

  • Quality Monitoring DashboardLive
    Rolls up school-level data for RTB and RTTI oversight and evidence-based decisions.
  • Teacher Certification LogLive
    Records completed pedagogy training per teacher, per school, per cohort.
  • Curriculum Alignment CheckWatch
    Flags drift between taught content and current labour-market skill needs.

School admins

  • ICT Asset RegisterLive
    Tracks equipment delivery, installation status and maintenance across the 11 schools.
  • Connectivity MonitorWatch
    Flags sites with power or network reliability issues undermining infrastructure investment.
  • Enrolment & Inclusion ReportLive
    Gender balance and social-inclusion reporting against school selection criteria.

Flags raised by school data

Generated from submitted records each time they change. These sit alongside the standing risk categories, not in place of them.

Standing risk categories

  • Connectivity and power reliabilityRural sites risk underusing ICT equipment if power and network access are not addressed alongside delivery.
  • Teacher retention after trainingDigital pedagogy skills are only durable if trained teachers remain in post through the project period.
  • Curriculum–labour market alignment driftContent can drift from evolving labour-market needs across the 44-month window.
  • Data system adoption after project closeMonitoring tools are only useful long-term if RTB and RTTI are resourced to maintain them.

Beneficiaries and partners

Primary beneficiaries

  • TVET students across 11 schools, gaining blended learning access
  • TVET teachers, equipped and trained in digital pedagogy
  • Private-sector employers, gaining better-prepared graduates
  • Technology companies expanding digital-learning services

Implementing and governing partners

  • Rwanda TVET Board (RTB) — lead implementing partner
  • Rwanda TVET Trainer Institute (RTTI) — teacher training delivery
  • Ministry of Education — strategic oversight and policy
  • LuxDev — manages the Luxembourg-funded contribution
